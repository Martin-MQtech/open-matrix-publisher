We started Open Matrix Publisher out of a small, recurring frustration: you create one video or write one article, and then you copy it by hand into a dozen different dashboards.

That workflow is slow, error-prone, and quietly discourages the consistency that actually builds an audience.

## Own the channel first

The first hard lesson came early. A brand-new account on a public community platform was flagged and locked the moment we connected automated tooling to it.

The platform was doing its job — protecting its feed from spam. But the experience made a principle obvious:

**Your content should live somewhere you control first. Then distribute.**

That is why this project treats a self-hosted, static site as the home base. A static site has no algorithm, no moderation queue, and no risk of losing your archive. It belongs to you.

## Publish locally, distribute globally

Open Matrix Publisher runs on your own machine. Your credentials never leave your computer.

- Write once, in Markdown.
- Publish to your own site.
- Distribute to the platforms where your audience actually is.

The distribution layer is a tool. The content is yours.

## What this journal is for

This space is the project's zero-risk channel: release notes, field notes on cross-border commerce, and research on hydrogen biology and advanced materials — published here first, mirrored elsewhere when it makes sense.

If you are reading this, the scaffold is working. Write your next post in Markdown, rebuild, and it will appear here.
